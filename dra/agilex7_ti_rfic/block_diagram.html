TI AFE7950

4TX / 4RX (+2 FB). 12 GSPS DAC, 3 GSPS ADC, integrated DUC/DDC/NCO/DSA. RF to 12 GHz. Space option: AFE7950-SP.

Agilex 7

Per slot: 1 FPGA + 2 AFEs (8 RF channels). Hosts DBFN, channelizer, DVB-RCS2 / DVB-S2X / 3GPP-NTN Low-PHY.

RX uplink

14.0–14.5 GHz needs Ku→IF super-het into AFE RX band. 8× AFE + 4× Agilex 7 for 32 beams.

TX downlink

10.7–12.75 GHz: direct RF DAC for most of band; optional IF→Ku for upper edge. 10× AFE + 5× Agilex 7 for 40 beams.
