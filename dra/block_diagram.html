RX / TX DRA Block Diagram

Ku-band Direct Radiating Array with separate RX and TX AGRW027/039 clusters, Direct-RF 64 Gsps converters, digital beamforming, and a 58 Gbps onboard router fabric.

RX uplink

14.0–14.5 GHz, 32 spot beams on 4 devices × 8 ADC channels. ADC → DDC → DBFN → channelizer → DVB-RCS2 / 3GPP-NTN Low-PHY.

TX downlink

10.7–12.75 GHz, 40 beams (NTN) / 32 (DVB-S2X) on 5 devices × 8 DAC channels. Framer → DBFN → DUC → Direct-RF DAC.

Isolation

9 devices total (5 TX + 4 RX) on physically separate silicon/RF paths for cleanest TX→RX isolation.

Control plane

Quad-core Arm Cortex-A53 HPS per device plus multi-device phase-sync IP for coherent beamforming.
